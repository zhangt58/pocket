Eight Bits of Trust; eMudhra's CA keys cracked in milliseconds

⭐ 138💬 54👤 hkc

A security researcher found that eight eMudhra certificate authority private keys were extremely weak single-byte values, allowing anyone to recover them from public certificates in milliseconds. The flaw affected six TLS issuing CAs and two code-signing CAs, prompting browsers to block the affected intermediates and remove or plan to remove the eMudhra root. Because the keys were inherently weak rather than stolen, the compromise could not be contained by revoking certificates alone.

⭐ 12💬 1👤 sohkamyung

Their adoption and later decline reflect how building codes balance safety, cost, and practicality, since owners rarely install safety features voluntarily and codes set minimum standards. #9

eMudhra intermediate CA compromise

⭐ 988💬 414👤 galadran

The private keys for eight eMudhra intermediate CA certificates have been compromised, allowing an attacker to potentially impersonate any website or service. The affected certificates chain to roots trusted by major browser and operating-system trust stores. Mozilla, Microsoft, Apple, and Chrome have blocked the certificates, and eMudhra has revoked them, though some clients may still need updates to stop trusting them.
