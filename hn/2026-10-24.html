⭐ 23💬 6👤 rex64

Its open-source code shows that the main Simulate function cycles through a 16-step counter, updating only selected systems such as traffic, fire, power, and crime each frame. This frame-slicing approach greatly reduced CPU load while keeping gameplay responsive enough for a city builder.
