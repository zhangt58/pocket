#8

European RISC-V Industrial Computing Initiative

⭐ 136💬 72👤 ymz5

The European RISC-V Industrial Computing Initiative is developing an open, non-commercial reference stack for industrial computers based on RVA23 RISC-V hardware. It defines and validates the interfaces between firmware, boot software, a partitioning hypervisor, real-time control, and Linux, so that compatible systems can run isolated workloads on a single SoC. ERICI aims to close the gap between available RISC-V silicon and the reliability, field connectivity, and long-term independence needed for industrial use.

#10

Vathographs: Photographic depth maps as creative expression

⭐ 28💬 6👤 hankbond

Vathography is a creative practice that transforms ordinary photographs into depth-based artworks by using AI to infer the relative distance of every point in a scene. Instead of relying on light and color, it treats volume as the primary element, allowing artists to remap focus and emphasize different distances through tone and contrast. This turns a normally hidden technical depth map into a new way of seeing and composing photographic subjects.
