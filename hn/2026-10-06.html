#5

Dust: Pretraining Transformers Without Backpropagation

⭐ 206💬 50👤 E-Reverance

Dust is a zeroth-order training method for transformer language models that replaces backpropagation by perturbing activations independently at each token and using a single forward pass to estimate gradients. It is competitive with backprop at large compute budgets and far more efficient than weight-space evolutionary search, with larger models showing better population efficiency. The method’s gradient estimates remain well aligned with backprop across scales up to 1B tokens, suggesting it could scale to larger pretraining.
