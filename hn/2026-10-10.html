WSL3 Performance is about 5-60% faster than WSL2 depending on the workload

⭐ 53💬 22👤 tonymet

Benchmarks comparing WSL 3.0.2 with WSL 2.6.3 show that WSL3 delivers workload-dependent performance gains, with the largest improvements in memory bandwidth and scheduler/IPC latency. Kernel microbenchmarks report up to about 61% faster memory throughput and around 10-12% lower scheduling latency, while basic syscall speed improves only modestly. In a real Go compilation test, WSL3 completed the build about 4% faster overall, with a larger reduction in kernel time.

Food processing influences metabolism and brain activity

⭐ 48💬 29👤 gmays

Researchers found that minimally and highly processed meals with nearly identical calories, macronutrients, water, salt, and weight produced different metabolic and brain responses. Ultraprocessed meals triggered greater insulin release, higher energy expenditure, less carbohydrate use, and altered activity in brain regions linked to motivation and reward. These results suggest food processing itself may influence metabolism and appetite, helping explain why ultraprocessed foods are associated with overconsumption and poor health outcomes.
