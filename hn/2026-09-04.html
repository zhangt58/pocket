Artificial beaver dams saw juvenile coho salmon survival rates go from 8% to 60%

⭐ 269💬 85👤 speckx

In California's Scott River valley, where 1830s fur trappers wiped out the beavers whose dams created coho salmon habitat, the Scott River Watershed Council built artificial beaver dams from posts, willow, conifer, and mud. The structures recreated about 9,000 square metres of cold wetland habitat, and juvenile coho survival on French Creek jumped from 8% before the dams to 60% after.
