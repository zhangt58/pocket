⭐ 47💬 9👤 DonAtimator

Harsher on Male? Evaluating LLMs on Gender-Asymmetric Moral Framing in Conflicts

⭐ 38💬 10👤 rzk

This study examines whether large language models judge identical negative behavior differently based on the actor's gender. Using a gender-mirrored benchmark of conflict scenarios, it finds that models consistently apply harsher, more punitive and blame-centered framing to male actors, while treating female actors with more empathy and therapeutic language. The pattern persists across model families, scenario types, and reasoning styles.
