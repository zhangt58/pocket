#6

Your TV can see through walls

⭐ 148💬 51👤 Curiositry

A simple radio receiver can detect movement behind walls by observing how a person's body reflects and interferes with a wideband TV signal. When the reflected signal cancels the direct signal at certain frequencies, the resulting fading pattern changes as the person moves, allowing the receiver to infer motion and even estimate walking speed. The same principle can be applied to other wideband signals such as WiFi or LTE.
