New 'Circadian' Subtype of Depression Affecting 1 in 4 Patients

⭐ 63💬 32👤 gmays

Researchers identified a circadian subtype of depression in about one quarter of patients, characterized by persistent jet-lag-like symptoms such as delayed sleep, social jet lag, evening chronotype, and morning fatigue. The study suggests depression is not a single disorder and that recognizing this subtype could help match patients to more effective treatments. It also found the subtype often began earlier and involved more severe features, including weight gain and higher risk of mania, psychosis, or suicidality.
